Every recommendation is sized from one multiple of the system: the multiplier your budget covers, capped at the published Multiplier.

Every recommendation runs on average monthly return on drawdown (RoDD/mo): net profit ÷ maximum drawdown over the window, shown per month of the record — a 54× RoDD over 8 months and a 32× RoDD over 16 months are not the same edge, and RoDD/mo is how this page tells them apart. Risk/trade = the typical losing trade at the shown multiplier (median loss); RoR/mo (return on risk) = average monthly profit divided by that risk.
