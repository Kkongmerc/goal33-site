Reading the stats: PF — profit factor (gross wins ÷ gross losses; above 1.3 is solid, above 2 is exceptional). Win — winning trade percentage. n — number of trades in the validation sample (bigger = more trustworthy). Net — net backtest profit over the sample at 1-contract NQ/ES/SI/GC scale. Max DD — worst peak-to-valley drawdown in the sample. RoDD — return over max drawdown (net profit ÷ worst drawdown over the sample). A stat not shown for a system is not part of its published validation record.
