How to read the stats
PF
Profit factor: gross wins ÷ gross losses. Above 1.3 is solid, above 2 is exceptional.
WIN
Winning trade percentage.
N
Number of trades in the validation sample. Bigger = more trustworthy.
NET
Net backtest profit over the sample at 1-contract NQ/ES/SI/GC scale.
MAX DD
Worst peak-to-valley drawdown in the sample.
RODD
Return over max drawdown: net profit ÷ worst drawdown over the sample.
STATstandout stat STATEST (estimate)conservative estimate pending final validation data STATverified

Every number on this site comes straight from verified broker backtests — commissions and slippage modeled — and is published exactly as the run produced it. Nothing smoothed, nothing cherry-picked, nothing inflated. Values marked EST are conservative estimates pending final validation data.

FAQ

Do I need a paid TradingView plan?

No. Invite-only scripts work on every TradingView tier, including the free plan. Higher tiers just give you more simultaneous alerts and charts.

How fast do I get access after buying?

Every purchase is delivered as a TradingView invite-only script, activated within 24h. Whop collects your TradingView username at checkout and the invite is issued to that account.

The invite-only script itself, activated within 24h, plus alert templates and setup docs. Updates are included for as long as you’re subscribed.

Can I automate these strategies?

Yes. Every system fires standard TradingView alerts. Wire them into your own webhook or execution tooling to automate, or trade them manually alongside the signals — the strategy works either way.

Is every system verified?

Yes. Every system goes through the research program’s validation runs before it gets listed, and the published stats come from those runs. Every Net figure on this site is the net backtest profit over the validation sample at 1-contract NQ/ES/SI/GC scale, shown with commissions and slippage modeled. See the full disclaimer at the bottom of this page.

Can I cancel?

No. All published figures are backtested or validation-run results, and past performance, real or simulated, never guarantees future results. Futures trading carries substantial risk of loss. Read the full risk disclosure at the bottom of this page before purchasing.
