FAQ

Do I need a paid TradingView plan?

No. Invite-only scripts work on every TradingView tier, including the free plan. Higher tiers just give you more simultaneous alerts and charts.

How fast do I get access after buying?

Access is granted automatically by Whop’s TradingView integration once you link your TradingView username at checkout, typically within a few minutes.

What exactly do I receive?

Invite-only access to the strategy script(s) on TradingView, alert templates, setup documentation, and ongoing updates for as long as you’re subscribed.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, in one click, from your Whop account. You keep access until the end of the billing period, then it’s revoked automatically.

Are the results guaranteed?

No. All published figures are backtested or hypothetical, and past performance, real or simulated, never guarantees future results. Futures trading carries substantial risk of loss. Read the full risk disclosure at the bottom of this page before purchasing.

Can I share the scripts with someone else?

Licenses are per TradingView username. Access granted to your account is for your use only; shared or resold access is revoked without refund.
